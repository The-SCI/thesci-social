## Overview

**TheSCI Social** (`https://social.thesci.co`) is our omnichannel AI social publishing, scheduling, and digital growth platform — engineered as a transparent, high-ROI alternative to **Hootsuite**, **Buffer**, and **Sprout Social**, and paired directly with our **28 Plain-English Digital Growth Modules** delivered through **[thesci.co.in](https://thesci.co.in)**.

## Platform & Managed Growth Highlights

- **Multi-Platform Publishing Hub:** Schedule and cross-publish posts, carousels, reels, and announcements across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and Google Business Profile from a single dashboard.
- **AI Content & Campaign Workflows:** Faster caption drafting, festival/exam-season calendars, and approval queues built for coaching institutes and SMB teams.
- **Platform-Capped Social Post Plans (India):** Starting at **`₹5,000/month`** for 1 social app and strictly capped at **`₹8,000/month`** across 5+ apps — paired with **28 modular growth add-ons** (Local SEO, AI Search `GEO`/`LLMO`, Reels/Video Production, Meta & Google Lead Ads, WhatsApp Funnels) and **8 Combo Saver Packs (`₹8,999–₹44,999/mo`)**.
